Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

ClipForge is a private tool used only by the owner of the Peak Stream Moments YouTube channel. It runs on the owner's own computer. There is no ClipForge server, and ClipForge is not offered to other users.

Google data ClipForge uses

  • YouTube (scope youtube): to read the name of the owner's channel, upload Shorts to it as private videos, update their titles and descriptions, and change a video's privacy only when the owner confirms it.
  • Gmail (scopes gmail.send and gmail.readonly): to send permission requests and one follow-up from the owner's Gmail address to streamers' public business contacts, and to read only those email threads to notice replies and delivery failures.

How the data is kept

  • Access tokens, and copies of permission requests and replies, are stored only on the owner's computer, in ClipForge's data folder.
  • No Google user data is sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone, and none is used for advertising.
  • Google user data is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • ClipForge's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Streamers' contact details

ClipForge collects only contact addresses that streamers publish themselves (for example in a channel description or link-in-bio page), uses them only to ask for permission to clip, and never writes again to anyone who says no. To be removed, reply "no" to the request or write to the channel's business email, listed on the Peak Stream Moments YouTube channel's About page.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect YouTube or Gmail in ClipForge at any time, or remove ClipForge's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.
